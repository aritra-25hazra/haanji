Ek chhoti si study

Aap HaanJi se teen chhote kaam karwayenge — jaise ek clinic mein phone karke appointment lena. Har kaam ke baad batayenge ho gaya ya nahi, aur aakhir mein das sawaal (1 se 5) aur do line apni raay.

Lagbhag 10 minute. Aapki awaaz record nahi hoti; sirf transcript, kaam ka natija aur aapke jawab save hote hain, bina naam ke. Aap kabhi bhi band kar sakte hain.

Teen kaam

Har kaam ke liye "Call shuru" dabaiye — demo nayi tab mein khulega. Kaam poora ho jaye (ya na ho paye) to wapas aakar bataiye. Mic ya type, jo aasaan lage.

Das sawaal

1 = bilkul sehmat nahi5 = poori tarah sehmat

Shukriya!
