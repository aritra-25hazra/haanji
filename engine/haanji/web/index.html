Frequently asked questions

Does it answer real phone calls?

On WhatsApp, yes: it connects straight to a number on Meta's WhatsApp Cloud API. The code for a phone line is ready too (a Twilio number, Deepgram and ElevenLabs voices), but it costs extra and needs your own keys. The browser demo runs on the mic.

Which languages does it speak?

Hinglish, plus Tanglish, Benglish, Marathinglish, Punglish, Tenglish and Gujlish: 10 packs and 7 languages today. Replies come in Roman script, the way people write on WhatsApp.

What if the agent does not understand, or the caller wants a person?

It never says what is not in the pack, prices included. If the caller asks for a person, or the job cannot be done on the call, it takes the number and the call goes onto the console's call-back list. You can also take over a live WhatsApp chat yourself.

What if a booking is wrong?

A booking is made only when the caller hears the slot and says yes, and both the agent's question and the caller's answer are signed into the receipt. A booking can be cancelled from the console's calendar, and the customer is told on WhatsApp.

Where do my data and my customers' data live?

On your own server; the code is open on GitHub. Calls are deleted after 180 days, and a customer's record can be erased at once when they ask.

What does the owner need?

Just a browser on a phone or laptop. The console has today's calls, the calendar, customers and settings; every new booking can also appear in your Google, Apple or Outlook calendar.
